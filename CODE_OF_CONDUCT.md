# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participating in this project to be a straightforward experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Standards

Things that keep this project worth contributing to:

- Assume the other person is trying to help.
- Critique the code, the design, or the argument — not the person.
- Say what you mean plainly, and accept plain feedback in return.
- When you are wrong, say so and move on.

Things that are not acceptable:

- Personal attacks, insults, or sustained hostility.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information without their explicit permission.
- Deliberately derailing discussions or wearing people down.

## Scope

This applies in every project space — issues, pull requests, discussions — and
whenever someone is representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour through GitHub's private report flow on the
repository, or by contacting a maintainer directly. Reports are handled
confidentially.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that do not meet this standard,
and may temporarily or permanently exclude anyone whose behaviour they judge
inappropriate. Maintainers who do not act in good faith may be removed from
the project.
